FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need money or experience to start?+
No experience required, and Julian covers how deals get funded with little or none of your own cash. What matters is doing the work.
Is this just theory like other courses?+
No. The whole point is that Julian actively flips and holds in Ohio. Everything is taught from real deals he has done, with real numbers.
Course or community, which is right for me?+
The course is best if you want a structured path to work through. The community is best if you want ongoing, live access as Julian does deals. Many people end up wanting both.
Will this make me money?+
No honest teacher can promise that. This gives you the system and the real-world numbers; results depend on your market, your capital, and the work you put in.
