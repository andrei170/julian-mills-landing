FAQ

Straight answers

What does it cost?+
It is a high-ticket, application-based program, priced accordingly. Exact investment is shared on the call once it is a mutual fit. (Final pricing confirmed with Julian.)
Do I need experience or capital?+
You do not need to be experienced, and Julian covers how deals get funded. You do need to be ready to act and invest in yourself.
How is this different from a course?+
You get direct access to Julian and a mastermind of serious investors, plus accountability. A course is information; this is mentorship and a room.
Will this make me money?+
No honest mentor can promise that. You get the playbook, the access, and the room. Results depend on your market, your capital, and the work you put in.
